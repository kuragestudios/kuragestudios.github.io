<a href="/games/just-maths">Just Maths</a> (formerly Just Maths Training) is back, and this time you don't need to install anything: the game now runs in your browser, for free.

What's new in this release:

- a **Daily challenge**: 16 fresh levels every day, from easy to hard, the same grid for everyone;
- the game plays in **portrait or landscape**, so it's at home on a phone as well as on a desktop;
- the in-app purchase is gone. Everything is unlocked.

The Mac, Windows and Android versions are on their way. Stay tuned!
