<a href="/games/block-hole">Block Hole</a> is back, and this time you don't need to install anything: the full game now runs in your browser, for free.

What's new in this release:

- **keyboard and mouse** controls next to the original swipes: arrow keys turn the block, W A S D orbit the camera;
- the game fills **any window**, portrait or landscape, so it's at home on a phone as well as on a desktop;
- 150 levels, six speeds from Zen to Master, and all the colours, exactly as you remember them.
