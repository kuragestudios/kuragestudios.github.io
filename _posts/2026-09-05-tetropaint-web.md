<a href="/games/tetropaint">Tetropaint</a> is back, and this time you don't need to install anything: the full game now runs in your browser, for free.

What's new in this release:

- the game plays in **portrait or landscape**, so it's at home on a phone as well as on a desktop;
- **mouse** controls next to the original touch gestures: click and drag to paint your tetrominos;
- unlimited levels, Classic and Zen modes, dark and light themes, all unlocked.

<a href="https://kuragestudios.github.io/tetropaint/play/">
	<img src="/images/web_play_now.svg" alt="Play in browser" width="256" />
</a>

If you enjoy it and want to say thanks, the Mac and Windows versions are available as a small paid download on itch.io. They play offline, full screen, at any window size, and they can host local Wi-Fi games for up to four players.

<a href="https://kuragestudios.itch.io/tetropaint">
	<img src="/images/itchio_get_it.svg" alt="Available on itch.io" width="256" />
</a>

The iOS and Android versions are still on the App Store and Google Play. Enjoy!
