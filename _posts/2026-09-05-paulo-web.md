<a href="/games/paulo">Paulo</a>, our very first game, is back after ten years. The bricks are still falling from the sky, and this time you don't need to install anything: the full game now runs in your browser, for free.

What's new in this release:

- **keyboard and gamepad controls**: arrow keys or A / D, or any gamepad, next to the original touch controls;
- the mobile **ads and the in-app purchase are gone**. Every level is open, the revive-by-ad button is history;
- the same hand-crafted pixel art, chiptunes and CRT filter, now at any window size.

</a>

If you enjoy it and want to say thanks, the Mac and Windows versions are available as a small paid download on itch.io. They play offline, full screen, at any window size.
