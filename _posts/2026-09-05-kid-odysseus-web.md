<a href="/games/kid-odysseus">Kid Odysseus</a> is back, and this time you don't need to install anything: the full game now runs in your browser, for free.

What's new in this release:

- the game plays in **portrait or landscape**, so it's at home on a phone as well as on a desktop;
- **keyboard and gamepad** controls next to the original swipes;
- all sixteen mazes, four colours and the CRT screen, exactly as you remember them.

<a href="https://kuragestudios.github.io/kid-odysseus/play/">

</a>

If you enjoy it and want to say thanks, the Mac and Windows versions are available as a small paid download on itch.io. They play offline, full screen, at any window size.
