<a href="/games/pipe-maze-3d">Pipe Maze 3D</a> is back, and this time you don't need to install anything: the game now runs in your browser, for free.

Mazes that are really in three dimensions. The pipes run up, down and through a box, a sphere or a pyramid: turn the shape around, find the flag, and run. A new free maze every day.

What's new in this release:

- rebuilt on Unity 6 for today's phones, tablets and browsers;
- four new **Scenery** themes — Cavern, Jungle, Lagoon and Twilight — bringing the count to 27;
- full **keyboard and mouse** controls: arrows or WASD to run, shift and a direction to turn the maze, Q and E to zoom, tab to switch viewpoint;
- the game plays in **portrait or landscape**, so it's at home on a phone as well as on a desktop.
