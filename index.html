Kurage Studios is a tiny independent game studio based in Japan. Since 2013, we have been crafting small, polished games across mobile, desktop, and the web, ranging from handcrafted pixel art arcade throwbacks to brain-bending 3D and AR puzzles.

We love the golden age of arcade games: chiptunes, the warm glow of CRT screens, and gameplay that is easy to pick up but difficult to master. Every title is designed, coded, and shipped with love by a very small team.
