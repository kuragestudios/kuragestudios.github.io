Kurage Studios is a tiny independent game studio based in Japan. Since 2013 we have been crafting small, polished games for iOS and Android — from hand-crafted pixel-art arcade throwbacks to brain-bending 3D and AR puzzles.

We love the golden age of arcade games: chiptunes, CRT scanlines, and gameplay that is easy to pick up but difficult to master. Every title is designed, coded, and shipped with love by a very small team (and one very supportive cat).
